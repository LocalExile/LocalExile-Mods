# Hello

The smallest mod: a chat command with arguments and the player's details.

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/hello [name]` | says hello to the name, or to the character, with its level, area and league |

Off by default: `/mod_enable hello` in game, or Waypoint's Mods section.

Works with: LocalExile v0.2.0-beta, v0.2.1-beta, v0.2.2-beta and v0.3.0-beta, and `main` from commit `1f6da7c0` (2026-11-03) on, the first with mod settings; not v0.1.0-beta.
