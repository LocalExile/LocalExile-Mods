# Item Rarity

A better chance of magic, rare and unique equipment from every kill, multiplied on top of the league's and other mods' rarity.

| Setting | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| `multiplier` | 10 | how many times the chance of magic, rare and unique equipment (1 to 100) |

Off by default: `/mod_enable itemrarity` in game, or Waypoint's Mods section.

Works with: LocalExile v0.2.0-beta, v0.2.1-beta and v0.2.2-beta, and `main` from commit `1f6da7c0` (2026-11-03) on, the first with mod settings; not v0.1.0-beta.
